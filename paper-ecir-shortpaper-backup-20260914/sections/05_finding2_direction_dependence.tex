\section{Finding 2: The Effect Differs Substantially Across Retrieval Direction}
\label{sec:finding2}

On MSCOCO, the identical checkpoints tell opposite stories depending on direction (Table~\ref{tab:main}). Image-to-text (I$\to$T, Dir.\ B) is unambiguous: vanilla is the \emph{only} variant with working I$\to$T retrieval ($10.30\%$ mean, $n{=}5$ seeds, std $0.67$); every regularized variant collapses to $\leq0.05\%$ mean, seed-independently, with std an order of magnitude smaller than vanilla's own. This holds even at weak regularization, where text-to-image (T$\to$I) is far less affected -- the two directions do not degrade together, or at comparable rates.

\paragraph{A natural fix -- decoupling regularization per target modality -- fails architecturally.} One might hypothesize a per-direction loss weight ($\lambda_{\text{img}}$/$\lambda_{\text{txt}}$ in place of one shared $\lambda$) could rescue both directions at once. We tested three settings on MSCOCO (3-seed means $\pm$ std): image-only ($\lambda_{\text{img}}{=}3,\lambda_{\text{txt}}{=}0$), image-dominant ($3/0.3$), and text-only ($0/3$). It does not work. I$\to$T stays collapsed in all three ($0.12\pm0.04\%$, $0.13\pm0.05\%$, $0.03\pm0.01\%$), including the exact-zero-text-weight setting; the text-only setting collapses I$\to$T as completely as regularizing both sides does. T$\to$I is \emph{worse than vanilla} in all three ($7.87\pm0.12\%$, $5.56\pm0.13\%$, $6.53\pm0.08\%$ vs.\ vanilla's $20.78\%$) -- worse than either uniform baseline, not merely worse than strong. This is not a tuning failure: image and text candidates share one encoder forward pass and one EMA-updated codebook at every RQ level, so a per-row loss weight has no mechanism to produce a per-modality effect. This is consistent with a direct measurement of the shared structure: under image-only regularization, text-side codes are visibly restructured despite exactly zero text-side loss weight ($566$ vs.\ $946$ unique level-1 code prefixes relative to vanilla), i.e.\ image-side regularization reaches text-side code structure through the shared codebook.

Section~\ref{sec:finding3} shows both this collapse and its resistance to per-modality decoupling already exist before decoder training begins, at the RQ tokenizer level.
